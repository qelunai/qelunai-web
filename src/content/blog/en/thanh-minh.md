Unlike most festivals, Thanh minh (Qingming) has no fixed lunar date: it is a **solar term**, set by the position of the Sun.

## When it falls

The Thanh minh term begins when the Sun reaches ecliptic longitude 15°, usually around 4 or 5 April, and lasts about 15 days. Because it follows the Sun, its lunar date changes every year. The start dates for the next three years, in Vietnam time:

| Year | Solar date | Lunar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2027 | Monday, 5 April 2027 | 29/2 Đinh Mùi |
| 2028 | Tuesday, 4 April 2028 | 10/3 Mậu Thân |
| 2029 | Wednesday, 4 April 2029 | 21/2 Kỷ Dậu |

In 2028, the start of Thanh minh falls on the same day as [Hùng Kings' Commemoration Day](/en/blog/gio-to-hung-vuong/). For how solar terms are worked out, see [The 24 solar terms](/en/blog/24-tiet-khi/).

## Meaning

"Thanh minh" means clear and bright. For Vietnamese people, it is mainly a time to remember the ancestors and look after family graves.

## Common customs

- **Tomb sweeping.** Families visit the graves, clear the grass, add fresh earth and light incense.
- **Offerings at home.** Afterwards the family goes home and cooks a meal to offer to the ancestors.

The sources do not agree on offerings for this occasion, so this post does not list them.

## Different places, different times

Tomb sweeping does not happen at the same time everywhere: some places do it during Thanh minh, others in the days before Lunar New Year.
