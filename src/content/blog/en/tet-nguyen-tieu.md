Fifteen days after Lunar New Year comes the first full-moon night of the new year: **Tết Nguyên tiêu**, also called the full moon of the first month or **Thượng nguyên**.

## When it falls

Tết Nguyên tiêu is the 15th day of the first lunar month. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Lunar date | Solar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | 15/1 | Saturday, 20 February 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | 15/1 | Wednesday, 9 February 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | 15/1 | Tuesday, 27 February 2029 |

How the full-moon day is worked out is explained in [Rằm and mùng một](/en/blog/ram-va-mung-mot/).

## Meaning

As the first full moon of the year, it is seen as a time to pray for the family's peace and for good crops all year.

## Common customs

- **Visiting the pagoda.** A Vietnamese saying goes that "praying to the Buddha all year is not equal to the full moon of the first month", which shows how much weight the day carries.
- **Offerings to the ancestors.** Back home, families make an offering to the ancestors.

## Meat or vegetarian

The sources differ on the offering tray: in some places it is a meat feast like the Tết feast, in others it is vegetarian. They do not tie this to particular regions, so this post does not list a specific tray. Follow your own family's custom.
