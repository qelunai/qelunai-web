The 10th day of the third lunar month is **Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương**, the day Vietnamese people remember the Hùng Kings.

## When it falls

Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương is the 10th day of the third lunar month. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Lunar date | Solar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | 10/3 | Friday, 16 April 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | 10/3 | Tuesday, 4 April 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | 10/3 | Monday, 23 April 2029 |

In 2028, the commemoration falls on the same day as the start of [Qingming](/en/blog/thanh-minh/).

## Public holiday

Under the 2019 Labour Code (No. 45/2019/QH14), as quoted by the Cần Thơ Department of Justice and the Hà Tĩnh provincial portal, employees get one paid day off on the 10th of the third lunar month. Check each year's official announcement for the exact holiday schedule.

## Meaning

The Hùng Kings are credited with founding the nation. The commemoration is a time for Vietnamese people everywhere to turn back to their roots.

## Common customs

- **Incense at the Hùng Temple.** Many people go to the Hùng Temple (Phú Thọ) to offer incense at this time.
- **Palanquin processions.** The festival includes palanquin processions.

## Common offerings

- **Bánh chưng** and **bánh giầy** (round sticky rice cake).
- **Fruit.**
