Tết Nguyên đán is the first day of the lunar year and the biggest festival of the year for Vietnamese people.

## When it falls

Tết Nguyên đán is the 1st day of the first lunar month; the three main days of Tết are the 1st, 2nd and 3rd. The solar dates for the next three years, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | Saturday, 6 February 2027 | Sunday, 7 February 2027 | Monday, 8 February 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | Wednesday, 26 January 2028 | Thursday, 27 January 2028 | Friday, 28 January 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | Tuesday, 13 February 2029 | Wednesday, 14 February 2029 | Thursday, 15 February 2029 |

Vietnamese Tết is sometimes one day off from the Chinese or Korean calendar, because the Vietnamese lunar calendar is worked out in Vietnam time (UTC+7). The post [Rằm and mùng một](/en/blog/ram-va-mung-mot/) explains why.

## Meaning

Tết opens a new year. It is a time for families to gather, for children who live far away to come home, to remember the ancestors together and to share wishes for a good year.

## Before Tết

- Cleaning the house and the ancestral altar.
- Setting a five-fruit tray (mâm ngũ quả) on the altar.

A week before Tết is [Kitchen Gods day](/en/blog/ong-cong-ong-tao/), and the last night of the year is [New Year's Eve](/en/blog/giao-thua/).

## The three days of Tết

- **Day 1:** children and grandchildren wish their grandparents and parents a happy new year; adults give lucky money (lì xì) to young children. Many families care about who **first-foots** the house (xông đất), that is, the first visitor to step in after the new year begins.
- **Days 2 and 3:** visiting relatives, teachers and friends.
- **Afternoon of day 3:** many families hold an offering to see the ancestors off, closing the main days of Tết.

## Common offerings

- **Bánh chưng** (square sticky rice cake).
- **A five-fruit tray.**
- **Candied fruit and sweets.**

The Tết feast, the flowers and the fruits on the five-fruit tray differ by region and by family. This post only lists what the sources mention in common.
