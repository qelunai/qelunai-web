On the last night of the lunar year, many families stay up until midnight to welcome **giao thừa** together, the moment the old year closes and the new one begins.

## When it falls

Giao thừa is the night of the last day of the twelfth month, turning into the 1st of the first month. The twelfth month has 29 days in some years and 30 in others, so its last day is not fixed. The next three, in Vietnam time:

| Old year → new year | Last day of the twelfth month | New Year's Eve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bính Ngọ → Đinh Mùi | 29/12 | Night of Friday 5 February 2027, into Saturday 6 February 2027 |
| Đinh Mùi → Mậu Thân | 29/12 | Night of Tuesday 25 January 2028, into Wednesday 26 January 2028 |
| Mậu Thân → Kỷ Dậu | 29/12 | Night of Monday 12 February 2029, into Tuesday 13 February 2029 |

In all three years the twelfth month has only 29 days, so there is no "30th of Tết". For why lunar months have 29 or 30 days, see [Rằm and mùng một](/en/blog/ram-va-mung-mot/).

## Meaning

Giao thừa is the handover between the old year and the new. The offering made at this moment is also called **lễ trừ tịch**, with the sense of setting aside the bad luck of the old year to welcome good things.

In the old view, this is also when the deity in charge of the old year hands the work over to the deity of the new year. The outdoor offering on New Year's Eve is linked to seeing off the old deity and welcoming the new one.

## Common customs

- **Offerings outdoors and indoors.** Many families set a tray in the yard or at the door, then make the offering to the ancestors inside.
- **Gathering for the new year.** The whole family sits together as the year turns.
- **Temple visits and lucky sprigs.** After midnight, some people visit a pagoda or temple and take home a small budding branch (hái lộc).

## Common offerings

The items the sources mention in common:

- incense and flowers;
- betel and areca;
- fruit;
- chicken (usually boiled);
- bánh chưng;
- rice wine;
- candied fruit and sweets.

The sources also note that the tray differs between families and regions. If your family has its own custom, keep to it.

## After giao thừa comes Tết

The 1st of the first month begins right after giao thừa. The customs of the three days of Tết are in the post [Lunar New Year](/en/blog/tet-nguyen-dan/).
