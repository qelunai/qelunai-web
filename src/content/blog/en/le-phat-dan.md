Every year in the fourth lunar month, pagodas put up flags and lanterns for **Lễ Phật đản**, the celebration of the birth of the Buddha Shakyamuni in the Lumbini garden.

## When it falls

The Vietnam Buddhist Sangha (Giáo hội Phật giáo Việt Nam, GHPGVN) holds the official celebration on the **15th day of the fourth lunar month**, the full moon. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | 8th of month 4 | 15th of month 4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | Thursday, 13 May 2027 | Thursday, 20 May 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | Tuesday, 2 May 2028 | Tuesday, 9 May 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | Sunday, 20 May 2029 | Sunday, 27 May 2029 |

The 8th is in the table because some traditions use it, as explained below. How the full-moon day is worked out is covered in [Rằm and mùng một](/en/blog/ram-va-mung-mot/).

## The 8th or the 15th?

Buddhist traditions do not all pick the same day:

- Some, such as the Northern (Mahayana) tradition, celebrate on the **8th of the fourth month**.
- Others, such as the Southern (Theravada) tradition, and the Buddha's Birthday recognised by many countries, use the **15th**.

In Vietnam, the GHPGVN takes the 15th as the main day. Under the Sangha's 2026 guidance, Buddha's Birthday week ran from the 8th to the 15th of the fourth month, so both days see activities.

## Common activities

- **Bathing the Buddha.** Pagodas and many Buddhist families hold a ceremony of bathing a statue of the infant Buddha.
- **Flags and lanterns.** Pagodas, Buddhist centres and Buddhist homes put up Buddhist flags, banners and lanterns.

The sources we compared do not give a common set of offerings for this day, so this post does not list any. If you plan to visit a pagoda, you can ask about its schedule for that year.
