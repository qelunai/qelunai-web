On the morning of the 5th day of the fifth lunar month, many Vietnamese families eat a bowl of fermented sticky rice (*cơm rượu nếp*) and some fruit as soon as they get up. This is **Tết Đoan ngọ**, known in folk custom as **tết diệt sâu bọ**, the pest-killing festival.

## When it falls

Tết Đoan ngọ is the 5th day of the fifth lunar month. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Lunar date | Solar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | 5/5 | Wednesday, 9 June 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | 5/5 | Sunday, 28 May 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | 5/5 | Saturday, 16 June 2029 |

The year Mậu Thân (2028) has a leap fifth month. Qelunai places Tết Đoan ngọ in the ordinary fifth month and does not repeat it in the leap month. For why leap months exist, see [Rằm and mùng một](/en/blog/ram-va-mung-mot/).

## Meaning

The name "pest-killing festival" sums up the wish behind the day: crops with few pests, and good health for the whole family.

## Common customs

- **An early breakfast.** On the morning of the 5th, people eat fermented sticky rice and fruit.
- **Picking leaves.** In some places people pick leaves at the hour of Ngọ (around midday), or boil leaves to make bath water. This is a folk custom; this post does not discuss whether it has any effect.
- **Offerings to the ancestors.** Families set out an offering tray for their ancestors.

## Common offerings

The items the sources agree on:

- fermented sticky rice;
- ash cakes (*bánh tro*, also called *bánh gio*);
- seasonal fruit, such as plums and lychees.

## Each region its own tray

According to two articles on Đoan ngọ offerings in the three regions:

- **The North** usually has plums, lychees, ash cakes and fermented sticky rice.
- **The Centre** has millet sweet soup (*chè kê*) and duck.
- **The South** has *bánh ú tro*, glutinous rice balls in syrup (*chè trôi nước*) and gấc sticky rice (*xôi gấc*).

The two articles do not match exactly: one splits the Centre into two areas with their own dishes. Every family has its own ways, so treat the list above as a reference only.
