On the 3rd day of the third lunar month, many Vietnamese families roll bánh trôi and cook bánh chay, glutinous rice balls, to place on the altar. This is **Tết Hàn thực**, the Cold Food Festival.

## When it falls

Tết Hàn thực is the 3rd day of the third lunar month. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Lunar date | Solar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | 3/3 | Friday, 9 April 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | 3/3 | Tuesday, 28 March 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | 3/3 | Monday, 16 April 2029 |

## Name and origin

"Hàn thực" means eating cold food. The festival comes from an old Chinese story.

In Vietnam, people do not avoid lighting the stove; they cook as usual. Tết Hàn thực is mainly a day for offerings to the ancestors.

## Common customs

- Families make bánh trôi and bánh chay together.
- The finished cakes are placed on the ancestral altar, then shared by the family.

## Common offerings

- **Bánh trôi.**
- **Bánh chay.**

If your family has its own custom, keep to it.
