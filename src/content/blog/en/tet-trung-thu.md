On the full moon of the eighth lunar month, children carry lanterns around the neighbourhood, lion-dance drums sound from the end of the lane, and the whole family sits down around a festive tray. This is **Tết Trung thu**, the Mid-Autumn Festival.

## When it falls

Tết Trung thu is the 15th day of the eighth lunar month. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Lunar date | Solar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | 15/8 | Wednesday, 15 September 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | 15/8 | Tuesday, 3 October 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | 15/8 | Saturday, 22 September 2029 |

How the full-moon day is worked out is covered in [Rằm and mùng một](/en/blog/ram-va-mung-mot/).

## Meaning

- **A festival for children.** In Vietnam, Trung thu has increasingly become a festival for children.
- **Reunion.** It is also a time for families to come together.

## Common customs

- **Lantern processions.** Children carry lanterns and go out to play on the full-moon night.
- **Lion dances.** Lion-dance troupes perform through the streets and villages.
- **Phá cỗ.** Children share the Mid-Autumn feast together.
- **Offerings to the ancestors.** Many families set out an offering tray for their ancestors.

## Common offerings

- **Mooncakes**: baked (*bánh nướng*) and soft sticky-rice (*bánh dẻo*).
- **Seasonal fruit.**
