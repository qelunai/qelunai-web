The full moon of the seventh lunar month goes by two familiar names: Buddhism calls it **Vu Lan**, and folk custom calls it **Tết Trung nguyên**.

## When it falls

Vu Lan is the 15th day of the seventh lunar month. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Lunar date | Solar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | 15/7 | Monday, 16 August 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | 15/7 | Sunday, 3 September 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | 15/7 | Friday, 24 August 2029 |

How the full-moon day is worked out is covered in [Rằm and mùng một](/en/blog/ram-va-mung-mot/).

## Meaning

- **Gratitude to parents.** In Buddhism, Vu Lan is a time to remember what we owe our parents and ancestors.
- **Pardon for the dead.** Folk custom also calls this day *xá tội vong nhân*, the day the souls of the dead are pardoned.

## Common customs

- **Offerings to the ancestors** at home.
- **Going to the pagoda.** Pagodas hold a Vu Lan ceremony and chant sutras.
- **Offerings to wandering souls** (*cúng chúng sinh*, *cúng cô hồn*). Many households set out a small tray in the late afternoon or evening for souls with no one to care for them.

The sources we compared do not agree on offerings, so this post does not list a specific tray. Follow your own family's ways.
