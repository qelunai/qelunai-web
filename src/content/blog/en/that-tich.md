The 7th day of the seventh lunar month is **Thất tịch**, which Vietnamese folk custom calls the day of "ông Ngâu and bà Ngâu". The rain at this time of year is called **mưa ngâu**, the Ngâu rains.

## When it falls

Thất tịch is the 7th day of the seventh lunar month. The next three dates, in Vietnam time:

| Lunar year | Lunar date | Solar date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Đinh Mùi | 7/7 | Sunday, 8 August 2027 |
| Mậu Thân | 7/7 | Saturday, 26 August 2028 |
| Kỷ Dậu | 7/7 | Thursday, 16 August 2029 |

Eight days after Thất tịch comes the full moon of the seventh month, [Vu Lan](/en/blog/le-vu-lan/).

## The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl

Thất tịch is linked to the tale of Ngưu Lang and Chức Nữ, the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl, a story of Chinese origin. The two lovers are kept apart and may meet only once a year, on this day. Vietnamese people call the pair ông Ngâu and bà Ngâu.

## Common customs

- **Visiting a pagoda.** Some people go to a pagoda to pray for a good match, or for harmony in the family.
- **Red-bean sweet soup.** In recent years many young people eat red-bean sweet soup (*chè đậu đỏ*) on this day. This is a new trend spread through social media, not an old custom.

The sources do not describe a set of offerings for Thất tịch, so this post does not list any.

## The "double" days

Like the 5th of the fifth month ([Tết Đoan ngọ](/en/blog/tet-doan-ngo/)), the 9th of the ninth ([Tết Trùng cửu](/en/blog/tet-trung-cuu/)) and the 10th of the tenth ([Tết Trùng thập](/en/blog/tet-trung-thap/)), Thất tịch is a day whose number matches its month.
